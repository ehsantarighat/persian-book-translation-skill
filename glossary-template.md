## Glossary Rules

1. Add every recurring name, organization, place, event, and technical term.
2. Use the same Persian equivalent throughout the project.
3. Do not change terms between chapters unless there is a strong reason.
4. If a better equivalent is chosen later, update previous usage mentally and keep consistency going forward.
5. For uncertain transliterations, preserve the original spelling.
6. For historical and political terms, choose precise equivalents based on context.
